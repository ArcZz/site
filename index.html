Research

How much fairness mitigation is actually necessary? My current research explores a generate-and-select approach: build inexpensive candidate models, then select one based on fairness and predictive performance. The goal is to understand when simple baselines can compete with specialized algorithms.

Interests: AI for SE · Data-Light Learning · robotics
